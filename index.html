The Food Journey

  1. Mouth: teeth chew food into a soft ball called a bolus.
  2. Esophagus: squeezing rings push the food down.
  3. Stomach: bump the Liver, Gallbladder, Pancreas and acid pockets to reach 100%.
  4. Pyloric gate: it only opens when food is fully churned into golden chyme.
  5. Small intestine: villi soak up vitamins and energy.
  6. Large intestine: soaks up water. The leftovers go to the toilet!
